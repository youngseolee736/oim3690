Looking back

My main takeaway

The biggest change was not just learning more technology. It was learning how to move from an idea to a tested product while making my own decisions.

How I changed as a builder

At first, I used AI mainly to generate pages and explain unfamiliar code. Over time, I became more selective: I compared references, rejected weak designs, tested generated code, fixed problems, and considered security, reliability, model quality, and cost.

Projects that show the journey

My work progressed from a client website to a JavaScript study tracker, an API-powered Pokémon project, and finally a multi-agent content-creation workflow.
