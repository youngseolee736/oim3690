Final Reflection

“I started the course by asking how a webpage appears online. By Week 10, I was deciding how a frontend, backend, APIs, and several AI agents should work together.”

My biggest change was learning to make my own decisions. Early in the course, I used AI mainly to generate pages and explain code. Over time, I learned to compare references, reject weak designs, test generated code, debug failures, and think about security, reliability, model quality, and cost. I now see web development as an iterative process: understand the problem, build a clear version, test it, and improve it with evidence.
